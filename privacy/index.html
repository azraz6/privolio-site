Privacy policy

A vault that stays yours.

Last updated 27 October 2026 · Applies to Privolio for iPhone and iPad, version 1.0

In one paragraph. Everything you add to Privolio — documents, scans, photos, recognized text, categories, dates, notes and reminders — is stored in the app's private container on your device. There is no Privolio account, no Privolio server, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no remote AI service. We could not read your documents if we wanted to, because they are never sent to us.

Who this policy is from

Privolio is an independently developed iOS and iPadOS app. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the developer of Privolio, and “the app” means Privolio. You can reach us at info@qshoppro.com.

What the app stores, and where

Privolio stores the information you choose to add in a database and file store inside its own sandbox on your device. That includes:

  • Document records you create or import: titles, dates, issuers, reference numbers, amounts, notes, tags and categories.
  • Attachments you add: PDFs, images and scanned pages, plus generated thumbnails.
  • Text recognized from attachments by Apple's Vision framework, stored locally so you can search it.
  • Reminder schedules and notification preferences, stored locally and scheduled through iOS.
  • App preferences such as appearance, app lock, expiry warning window and recent searches.

None of this leaves your device through the app. There is no server run by us for the app to send it to.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your documents, recognized text, notes, dates or any copy of them.
  • We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not include third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not require an email address, phone number, name or login to use the app.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not receive it.

The app writes ordinary diagnostic messages to the device's own system log. Those messages stay on the device and are not transmitted by Privolio.

Permissions the app may ask for

iOS shows a prompt the first time the app needs one of these. You can decline any of them, and change your mind later in iOS Settings.

Text recognition

When you scan or import a document, Privolio can read its text using Apple's Vision framework on the device. The image and the resulting text are processed locally and are not uploaded. OCR is limited to 40 PDF pages and 200,000 characters per document. Recognized text is never included in notification previews.

Purchases

Privolio Pro is sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes the payment and provides the app with verified entitlement information. Privolio does not receive your payment card details. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Backups and the one way data can leave the device

Privolio excludes its vault from iCloud device backup. A backup exists only when you explicitly export one from Settings. The exported .privoliovault file contains your records and attachments and is not encrypted; anyone with the file can read its contents. Store it somewhere you trust. The app checks a SHA-256 integrity value when exporting and restoring.

When you share an export, a document summary or a file, the app hands it to the iOS share sheet and you choose the destination. Once you send a copy somewhere, that copy is governed by the service you sent it to.

Security

Your data is protected by the app sandbox and by iOS device encryption and passcode. You can require Face ID, Touch ID, Optic ID or your device passcode before Privolio opens. App lock is an access screen; it does not add a separate layer of encryption to your documents. No app can protect data on a device that is already unlocked and in someone else's hands.

Keeping and deleting your information

We hold no copy of your information, so there is nothing for us to retain. On your device, data stays until you delete it. You can delete individual documents in the app, or use Settings → Privacy & security → Erase all vault data to remove the database, attachments, thumbnails and pending reminders. Deleting the app also deletes its container.

Children

Privolio is a personal paperwork tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a future version adds a feature that changes how data is handled, the policy will be updated before that version ships.
